## 1. What is Accounting?

Accounting is the information system that identifies, records, and communicates the economic events of an organization to interested users.

### Three Activities of Accounting

| Activity | Meaning |
|----------|---------|
| Identify | Identify economic events |
| Record | Record the economic events |
| Communicate | Communicate accounting information to interested users |


## 2. Users of Accounting Information

Users of accounting information are divided into two types:

### Internal Users

Internal users are people inside the organization who use accounting information for decision-making.

Examples:

- Managers
- Chairman
- Administrators
- Labor Union

### External Users

External users are people or organizations outside the business who need accounting information.

Examples:

- Investors
- Creditors
- Customers
- Taxing Authorities
- Regulators
- Others

## 3. Accounting Equation

The basic accounting equation is:

```txt
Assets = Liabilities + Owner's Equity
```

Formula:

```txt
A = L + OE
```

Where:

```txt
A  = Assets
L  = Liabilities
OE = Owner's Equity
```

### Example

```txt
Assets          = 10,000
Liabilities     = 3,000
Owner's Equity  = 7,000

10,000 = 3,000 + 7,000
```

Therefore, the accounting equation is balanced.


## 4. Assets

Assets are the economic resources owned or controlled by a business.

Examples:

- Cash
- Bank
- Building
- Furniture
- Land
- Vehicles
- Machines
- Office Equipment
- Supplies
- Investment
- Accounts Receivable

### Accounts Receivable

Accounts Receivable means money owed to the business.

Example:

A customer buys goods but does not pay immediately. The amount the customer owes the business is Accounts Receivable.


## 5. Liabilities

Liabilities are the claims of creditors against the assets of a business.

Examples:

- Bank Loan
- Accounts Payable
- Rent Payable
- Tax Payable
- Notes Payable

### Accounts Payable

Accounts Payable means money that the business owes to others.


## 6. Owner's Equity

Owner's Equity is the owner's claim on the assets of the business.

Formula:

```txt
Owner's Equity = Assets - Liabilities
```

### Example

```txt
Assets      = 100,000
Liabilities = 30,000

Owner's Equity = 100,000 - 30,000
               = 70,000
```


## 7. Expanded Owner's Equity Equation

Owner's Equity can be expressed as:

```txt
OE = Capital - Drawings + Revenue - Expenses
```

Where:

```txt
OC = Owner's Capital
OD = Owner's Drawings
R  = Revenue
E  = Expenses
```

Therefore:

```txt
A = L + OC - OD + R - E
```

### Owner's Capital

Owner's Capital is the money or assets invested by the owner in the business.

### Owner's Drawings

Drawings are cash or other assets withdrawn by the owner for personal use.

## 8. Revenue

Revenue is the income earned by a business from its normal business activities.

Examples:

- Sales Revenue
- Service Revenue
- Rent Revenue

### Effect on Owner's Equity

Revenue increases Owner's Equity.


## 9. Expenses

Expenses are the costs incurred to earn revenue.

Examples:

- Salary Expense
- Tax Expense
- Utility Expense
- Transportation Expense
- Supplies Expense
- Advertisement Expense
- Insurance Expense
- Depreciation Expense

### Effect on Owner's Equity

Expenses decrease Owner's Equity.


## 10. Depreciation Expense

Depreciation Expense is the cost of using an asset over its useful life.

Example:

A business uses a mobile phone, machine, or vehicle for several years. Its cost is allocated over the period it is used.

This allocated cost is called Depreciation Expense.



## 11. Revenue vs Expense

| Revenue | Expense |
|---------|---------|
| Income earned by the business | Cost incurred by the business |
| Increases Owner's Equity | Decreases Owner's Equity |
| Sales Revenue | Salary Expense |
| Service Revenue | Utility Expense |
| Rent Revenue | Advertisement Expense |



## 12. Accounts Receivable vs Accounts Payable

| Accounts Receivable | Accounts Payable |
|---------------------|------------------|
| Money owed to the business | Money owed by the business |
| Business will receive money | Business will pay money |
| It is an Asset | It is a Liability |


## Key Formulas

```txt
Assets = Liabilities + Owner's Equity

A = L + OE

Owner's Equity = Assets - Liabilities

OE = Capital - Drawings + Revenue - Expenses

A = L + OC - OD + R - E
```



## Quick Revision

- Accounting has three activities: Identify, Record, Communicate.
- Internal users work inside the organization.
- External users are outside parties who need accounting information.
- Assets are resources owned by the business.
- Liabilities are amounts owed by the business.
- Owner's Equity is the owner's claim on business assets.
- Revenue increases Owner's Equity.
- Expenses decrease Owner's Equity.
- Accounts Receivable is an asset.
- Accounts Payable is a liability.
